# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router, TypeScript, React, Tailwind CSS v4, typed local content, Vitest, and Playwright.

## Users

Primary users are engineering hiring teams evaluating Felix for senior full-stack, cross-platform, or systems-oriented roles. Technical collaborators are a secondary audience looking to understand architecture, delivery approach, and project evidence.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio makes Felix's engineering approach, project context, and public-safe evidence easy to evaluate. Success means a hiring team or technical collaborator can quickly understand the kinds of problems Felix can enter, how he reasons about them, and how to continue the conversation.

## Positioning

An adaptable, generalist full-stack engineering portfolio: Felix can move across application stacks and system boundaries, execute quickly in unfamiliar domains, and use AI-assisted workflows as part of reviewed, validated engineering delivery. The current evidence is strongest for Flutter/Dart, connected systems, architecture, testing, and delivery enablement; other stack experience must remain clearly corroborated before being presented as verified.

## Operating Context

Visitors scan the portfolio during hiring evaluation or technical collaboration. They need concise orientation first, then progressively deeper project, capability, and engineering-story evidence. The site is static/public-facing and uses approved local content rather than a CMS or authenticated workflow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve public-safe claims only; do not invent employers, clients, metrics, links, confidential details, or unsupported implementation experience.
- Current verified evidence includes Flutter, Dart, BLoC/Cubit, AES70/OCA concepts, binary protocols, WebSocket-backed repositories, state ownership, recovery-aware workflows, testing, Linux test doubles, GitHub Actions references, and AI-assisted workflow implementation with engineering review.
- C#, React, TypeScript/JavaScript, Dart/Flutter, native bindings, and other stack breadth may be part of future positioning, but specific experience must be corroborated before being stated as verified personal experience.
- Keep route and publication boundaries intact. Draft and unverified material must not enter production navigation or metadata.
- Preserve the existing Next.js App Router, typed local content model, and semantic route contracts.

## Brand Commitments

- Felix Edrian Ybañez is the existing public name and identity.
- Preserve the established dark, documentation-inspired visual language unless a future redesign explicitly changes it.
- Keep the voice credible, warm, precise, calm, and evidence-oriented; avoid hype and unsupported ownership claims.
- Preserve approved imagery and public-safe content boundaries documented in `docs/portfolio-context.md` and related content inventories.

## Evidence on Hand

- Typed project, capability, and engineering-story content in `src/lib` and `docs/contents`.
- Public-safe project and portrait imagery under `public/images`.
- Published teammate feedback in `src/data/testimonials.ts` and `docs/testimonial-submissions`.
- Factual boundaries and verified/unverified technology mapping in `docs/portfolio-context.md`.
- No verified employment chronology, résumé, public contact links, quantified outcomes, customer identities, or confidential implementation details; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Lead with the engineering problems and boundaries, not a tool list.
- Make evidence easy to scan and deeper reasoning easy to reach.
- Preserve user trust through explicit uncertainty and public-safe claims.
- Show adaptability without overstating unverified experience.
- Treat accessibility, responsive behavior, and maintainable route boundaries as product quality.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic HTML, meaningful labels, keyboard access, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion behavior across the public site.
