# Testimonial from Mark Vincent Tangpus

## Testimony

Felix was one of the friendliest and most approachable people I worked with at Kyocera. He was easy to work with, always open to ideas, and made collaboration feel comfortable—even when we were working through difficult technical problems or tight requirements.

What stood out to me was his creativity as an engineer. I especially remember the work he did on the Device Settings experience, where he created a more customizable UI instead of settling for a rigid, one-size-fits-all approach. He looked for a practical solution that could better adapt to the product's needs, and he was able to turn that idea into something useful for the team and the users.

Felix is also very down to earth. He communicates naturally, listens to other people's perspectives, and does not make collaboration feel complicated. He brings a positive attitude to the work, shares ideas freely, and is someone you can rely on when solving problems together.

Overall, Felix is a creative and dependable software engineer who is friendly, collaborative, and easy to work with. I would be happy to work with him again.
