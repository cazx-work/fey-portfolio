## Delivery notes

- Aim for a calm, conversational pace rather than reading every word with equal emphasis.
- Pause briefly after your name, after “system complexity,” and before the final closing.
- Emphasize these phrases: “system complexity,” “typed protocol layer,” “preserving configuration state,” and “software people can operate with confidence.”
- Keep the camera introduction personal. The technical example should support who you are, not become a product demo.
- Remove the final connection line if the video is embedded directly beside a contact button.
